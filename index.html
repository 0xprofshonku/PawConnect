THE PROBLEM

Street dogs need help. Sometimes, they need it quickly.

Injuries, illness, extreme weather and lack of food can put street dogs at risk. But people often don't know who to contact.

🩹

Injuries

Injured dogs may need immediate veterinary attention.

🥶

Cold Weather

Winter can be especially difficult for dogs living on the streets.

🥣

Lack of Food

Many street dogs struggle to find enough food and clean water.

🏠

No Shelter

Dogs often have nowhere safe to escape extreme weather.
